# DeepScreen search and growth roadmap

## Shipped
- Page-specific metadata, social cards, canonicals and structured data
- Open stock ratios, scores, verdicts, summaries, FAQs and breadcrumbs
- Ratio glossary, rankings, sectors, stock comparisons and options explainers
- About, methodology, crawler policy, sitemap and `llms.txt`
- Newsletter capture and a non-blocking repeat-visitor account prompt
- Five-day refund policy and founder contact details

## Backlink plan (low budget)
1. Publish one data-led exchange or sector study monthly and pitch its findings to finance newsletters and market-data journalists.
2. Offer glossary definitions and methodology citations to university investment clubs, finance educators and relevant resource pages.
3. Create embeddable, attributed ranking tables for high-quality personal-finance publications; avoid paid link schemes and bulk directories.
4. Submit DeepScreen to legitimate product, fintech and open-data directories, then reclaim unlinked brand mentions.
5. Maintain a public corrections process so researchers can confidently cite stable URLs.

## Deferred
- Read-only per-stock JSON endpoints, after versioning, rate limits, data licensing and cache policy are designed.
- Never publish `/compare/deepscreen-vs-screener-in`; Indian filing ratios may rely on Screener.in, making that comparison misleading.
