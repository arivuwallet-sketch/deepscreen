## Editorial intent

### Save Money
Search intent: users who are paying only the minimum amount due on a credit card and need to understand the real cost and build a practical repayment exit.

Primary decisions:
- minimum amount due versus total amount due
- APR and finance-charge implications
- how much above the minimum can be paid safely
- prioritising multiple revolving balances
- when refinancing actually reduces total cost

Primary source:
- Reserve Bank of India — Credit Card and Debit Card: Issuance and Conduct Directions, 2022, as updated

### Protect Money
Search intent: policyholders considering switching health insurers who need to preserve waiting-period and moratorium continuity while comparing real protection.

Primary decisions:
- what portability transfers
- how the 60-month moratorium works
- how enhanced sums insured affect continuity
- comparing room limits, co-pay, deductible and hospital network
- avoiding an uninsured gap

Primary source:
- Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India — Health Department consumer FAQs

### Make More Money
Search intent: creators, freelancers and small businesses looking for legitimate ways to monetise intellectual property they actually own.

Primary decisions:
- whether the asset is owned and reusable
- copyright versus trademark, patent or design
- sale, licence, subscription, SaaS, royalty or service-plus-IP model
- whether registration is commercially justified
- how to test real customer demand before spending heavily on protection

Primary sources:
- Copyright Office, Government of India — FAQ, Copyright Act Chapter X and fee schedule
- IP India — Trade Marks forms and official fees
