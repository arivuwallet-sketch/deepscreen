## Editorial rules

- Use answer-first intros and descriptive H2s.
- Prefer official Indian primary sources for regulatory or protection claims: RBI, SEBI, DICGC, IRDAI, Ministry of MSME and other relevant government/regulator sources.
- Make clear what is fact, illustration, framework or opinion.
- Do not promise returns, guaranteed passive income or wealth outcomes.
- Do not publish unverified tax rates, investment returns, property yields or business margins.
- Treat stocks, real estate and business as different ways of deploying time/capital, not interchangeable products.
- Each article must include at least one original framework, calculation, decision table, checklist or worked example.
- Link the three daily articles to one another when relevant.
- Keep visible FAQ content aligned with FAQPage structured data generated by the existing blog route.
- Do not change dateModified unless the page has received a substantive editorial update.
